OOTD Today — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.5 and later

Short version. The app is local-first: your wardrobe, outfits, calendar and logs stay on your phone. We run no analytics, no advertising and no account system. The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly trigger: a weather query, an optional photo you confirm for garment analysis, an optional chat message to an AI provider you configure, and an optional model download.

1. Who we are

OOTD Today is an open-source Android application maintained by an individual developer (github.com/xunhua666/ootd-today). For any privacy question or request, open an issue in that repository.

Weather

The app requests approximate (city-level) coordinates and sends them to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. If you deny location permission, a default city is used instead.

Garment photos (optional)

When you choose to analyse a garment, the app asks for confirmation before uploading one photo. The image is resized to a maximum edge of 1280 px, rotated according to EXIF, and re-encoded as JPEG without the original EXIF metadata. It is then sent to the vision endpoint you configured (for example an OpenAI-compatible vision model). The result is a list of candidate garment attributes that you review and confirm before saving. Photos are never uploaded unless you confirm, and the app does not send them to the developer.

AI expert (optional)

There are two modes. The cloud expert sends your prompt, a text summary of your wardrobe (name, category, colour, style, confirmed garment shape, occasions) and, if you enable it, selected garment photos to the chat-completions endpoint you configured. The local expert runs a Qwen3-1.7B model on your phone and does not upload wardrobe data or chat text. In both modes the app states clearly that the content is AI-generated and may be inaccurate.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or name.
  • No analytics, advertising SDK, device identifiers or usage tracking.
  • No crash-reporting SDK that uploads data to us.
  • No training of models on your wardrobe, photos or conversations.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.

5. Security

All network requests from the app require HTTPS; plain HTTP is rejected except for loopback addresses used during local development. API keys are encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore. On devices where the Keystore is unavailable, the app falls back to a locally encoded value in private app storage and does not claim hardware-backed protection.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. There is no server-side copy held by the developer, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Third-party AI providers you configure have their own retention policies; please review the terms of the provider you choose.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. The intended audience is adults.

8. International users

Because the app has no backend, the developer does not transfer your data internationally. Data sent to Open-Meteo, Hugging Face or your configured AI provider is handled by those providers under their own terms, which may involve processing outside your country.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and the date at the top will be revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: open an issue in the project repository.
